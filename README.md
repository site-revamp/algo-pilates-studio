# ALGO PILATES – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

ALGO PILATES is a premium Pilates studio brand based in Dubai, specialising in expert-led Reformer Pilates and athletic-inspired movement training. Its services include group classes, private training, Tower classes, workshops, and online Pilates content.

The redesigned homepage focuses on communicating the studio's distinctive combination of Pilates precision, athletic strength, and progressive movement. It is structured to help beginners and experienced practitioners understand the available classes, explore studio locations, and move towards booking.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger Brand Positioning
- Made the combination of traditional Pilates principles and athletic-inspired Reformer training more prominent.
- Established a clearer brand message around precision, strength, and progressive movement.
- Addressed the previous lack of visibility for ALGO PILATES' distinctive training approach.
- Strengthened the distinction between the studio and conventional Pilates businesses.

### 2. Clearer Class Presentation
- Improved the organisation of the main class formats: Movement Principles, Athletic Reformer, and Reformer Gym Vibes.
- Made the differences between class types easier to understand through clearer descriptions and structured presentation.
- Improved the visibility of additional offerings, including Tower classes, private training, and Pilates for teenagers.
- Addressed the difficulty visitors may face when choosing a suitable class.

### 3. Improved Studio Discovery and Booking Journey
- Gave greater visibility to the two studio locations: Dubai Investment Park and Motor City.
- Improved the presentation of location information to help visitors identify their preferred studio.
- Created a clearer pathway from studio discovery to class schedules and booking.
- Addressed unnecessary navigation between finding a location and accessing the booking process.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Established a more distinctive fitness-focused visual direction reflecting ALGO PILATES' athletic-inspired training identity.
- **Content Hierarchy:** Improved the sequence of homepage sections, introducing brand positioning before class discovery and studio information.
- **Class Navigation:** Organised class formats into clearer categories to make programme selection easier.
- **User Experience:** Improved the visitor journey from understanding the training approach to exploring classes and booking.
- **Location Visibility:** Made both studio destinations easier to identify and access.
- **Brand Consistency:** Connected the messaging, class presentation, and overall homepage structure around Pilates precision, athletic strength, and progressive movement.

---

**Project Focus:** Distinctive brand positioning, clearer class selection, and a smoother studio discovery and booking experience.
